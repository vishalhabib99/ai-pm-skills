PASS if the plan identifies wrongly approving an ineligible or fraudulent refund as the most costly error AND sets at least one launch gate specifically on that error (for example precision of auto-approvals, or a maximum false-approval rate), not only overall accuracy.
FAIL if the plan's only launch gates are overall accuracy or speed, or if it treats wrong approvals and wrong escalations as equally costly without saying so.
